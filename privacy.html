Privacy Policy for GymTracks

Last updated: March 8, 2026

GymTracks ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use the GymTracks mobile application (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using GymTracks, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

GymTracks is designed with a privacy-first approach. Most of your data remains strictly on your device.

A. Workout and Fitness Data

  • Exercise Logs & Workouts: Records of exercises, sets, reps, weights, duration, and workout routines you input into the App.
  • Body Measurement Data: Body weight and related physical progress logs you manually enter.

B. Health and Activity Data (Health Connect / Activity Recognition)

  • Step Count & Activity: If you grant permission, GymTracks accesses step count data via Health Connect (android.permission.health.READ_STEPS) and physical activity status (android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION).
  • Usage: Step data is used strictly to display your daily physical activity and progress within the App's steps dashboard.

C. Google Account & Cloud Sync Data

  • Google Authentication: If you choose to enable Cloud Sync, GymTracks uses Google Sign-In to authenticate your Google account.
  • Google Drive Access: GymTracks uses Google Drive API permissions to store and retrieve your encrypted/formatted workout backup files within your personal Google Drive storage.
  • Note: We do not collect or store your Google account password or personal details on any external servers.

D. Device Permissions

  • Notifications: Used to send local workout reminders and weight logging reminders scheduled by you.
  • Internet: Used exclusively for authenticating with Google and syncing backups to your Google Drive account.
  • Boot Receiver: Used to reschedule local notification alarms if your device reboots.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  1. Core Functionality: To record, calculate, and present your workout logs, progress charts, and step activity.
  2. Personal Backup & Sync: To upload and restore your workout history to your private Google Drive account upon your explicit request.
  3. Local Reminders: To trigger push notifications on your device for scheduled workouts and weight tracking.

We do not:

  • Sell, rent, or trade your personal, health, or workout data to third parties.
  • Use your health or fitness data for advertising, marketing, or data mining purposes.
  • Track your location or build user profiles across external websites or services.

3. Storage and Protection of Data

  • Local On-Device Storage: All workout routines, logs, step counts, and settings are stored locally on your device using a secure Room database.
  • Google Drive Storage: Cloud backups reside strictly within your personal Google Drive storage. Only you have control over your Google Drive files.
  • No External Servers: GymTracks does not operate remote database servers to collect or retain your fitness data.

4. Third-Party Services and SDKs

GymTracks integrates with limited third-party services necessary for app features:

  1. Google Play Services & Google Drive API: Used for user authentication and backup synchronization. Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy
  2. Android Health Connect: Used for reading step count data locally on your device.

5. Google API & Health Connect Limited Use Disclosures

GymTracks's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs (including Google Drive and Health Connect) will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy and the Health Connect Developer Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • Health Connect data is processed locally on your device and is never transferred to third parties or used for ad targeting.
  • Google Drive permissions are restricted strictly to creating and accessing GymTracks backup files created specifically by the App.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You retain full control over your data:

  • In-App Data Deletion: You can delete individual exercises, workouts, or reset your data at any time from within the App settings/history.
  • Clearing Local Data: You can delete all local App data at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > GymTracks > Storage > Clear Data).
  • Deleting Cloud Backups: You can manage or delete your backup files directly from your Google Drive account or through the App's Drive Sync screen.
  • Uninstalling: Uninstalling GymTracks deletes all local database files stored on your device.

7. Children's Privacy

GymTracks does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
